# SOLACE Garden — Milestone History

**Created:** 2026-11-21  
**Purpose:** Durable archive of accepted SOLACE Garden milestones as they rotate out of the SOLACE Network monday.com board.

## Policy

See the [SOLACE project-management policy](https://github.com/solacenetwork/.github/blob/main/docs/PROJECT_MANAGEMENT.md). The [master roadmap](ROADMAP.md) retains the full approved sequence, while the [UI redesign specification](UI_REDESIGN.md) governs the current redesign work. monday.com handles the active execution window.

An item is archived here **only after developer acceptance**. Record the original milestone ID, acceptance date, outcome, evidence (commits/PRs/tests), specification links, and caveats before removing its monday.com counterpart. Preserve any unique monday.com notes that matter. Do not invent dates or claim tests were performed without evidence.

**Important identifier distinction:** `GARDEN-UI-8A4` is part of the *post-8F UI redesign* and is unrelated to `GARDEN-V1-8A` (Storage Foundations). After UI redesign Step 10 is accepted, feature development resumes at `GARDEN-V1-8G`.

## Historical evidence pending consolidation

The existing [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) and [UI_REDESIGN.md](UI_REDESIGN.md) already contain substantial completion records for earlier work, including the accepted **UI 8A1–8A3**. These are retained in place for now and **not duplicated or removed** during the monday.com pilot. A later, separately reviewed archival pass may transfer those records into this file without loss.
